What is the IT Industry?

The Information Technology industry includes organizations and professionals involved in creating, managing, storing, processing and protecting digital information.

Modern IT is not limited to writing code. It includes software engineering, networking, cloud infrastructure, data analysis, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, technical support, product management and many other specialized areas.

Almost every modern industry depends on IT, including banking, healthcare, education, transportation, entertainment, manufacturing and retail.

Why is IT Important?

Technology helps organizations automate processes, communicate globally, analyze information, reduce costs, improve customer experiences and create completely new products and services.

This makes IT one of the most important drivers of digital transformation and economic growth.
